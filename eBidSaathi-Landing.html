The Problem

MSMEs are losing contracts they never even knew existed

India's procurement system is fragmented across dozens of portals. By the time most businesses find a relevant tender, the deadline has passed.

  • 🔍

    Tenders are scattered across 54+ portals

    Central, state, PSU and defence portals all publish independently. No MSME has the bandwidth to monitor all of them daily.

  • 📄

    Tender documents are complex and time-consuming

    Eligibility criteria, EMD requirements, and document checklists take hours to decode — and one missing paper means instant disqualification.

  • ⏰

    Deadlines move fast — corrigenda move faster

    Tender conditions change after publication. Businesses that miss the amendment bid on wrong terms, wasting effort and sometimes money.

  • 🧑‍💼

    No dedicated tender team

    Hiring a full-time tender executive costs ₹25,000–₹50,000/month. Most MSMEs can't justify it — even when contracts are worth crores.
